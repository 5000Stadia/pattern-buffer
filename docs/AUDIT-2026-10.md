# Self-audit, October 2026: intelligence and elegance of the structures

**Why.** Kabe, 2026-10-08: *"pattern buffer and construct were made with weaker
models they may want to do a self audit on the intelligence and elegence of the
structures for self directed polishing."* The exit, set before starting: one
pass over each part, this ranked note, and the top fixes done.

**How.** Five independent auditors, each reading one part of the engine in full,
read-only:
- the log core;
- folds and reads;
- the ingest gate and classifier;
- identity;
- the public surfaces.

Every P1 was reproduced with a script before it was ranked. pb reviewed every
finding and checked the cross-part overlaps. Behaviour-preserving fixes landed on
branch `polish/audit-2026-10`. Everything that changes an answer a host gets is
listed below as a proposal, never built past this note.

**Verdict.** The design holds. Two things held in all five parts:
- the founding invariants (one append-only log; frames filtered at source in the
  read paths; nothing derivable stored);
- the porcelain/MCP boundary.

What the weaker-model build left is **unevenness**. A rule is applied on one path
and missing on its sibling: a gate copied eight times, with the copies drifted
apart. Four value comparators disagree. Reads skip the canonicalization that
writes apply. Evidence rank governs one durability class and not the others.
Most of the real bugs live in those gaps. The second theme is **per-call
overhead**: identity, head and durability are recomputed on every read.

## P1: wrong answers or broken invariants, ranked

Each fix is small, under about 40 lines. But each changes what a host sees, so
each goes spec → pbr → build, grouped into five batches (next section).

0. **`holds`/`wearing` invert direction, and it is live in host worlds.**
   - The aliases rename the attribute without swapping subject and object.
   - The correctly-directed repair is then refused as a containment cycle, and
     old receipts replay the bad alias even if the entry is deleted.
   - Construct's own extraction path reaches it: 10 of 165 construct world files
     carry the receipt. Live example: `person:callum held_by
     doc:school_archive_folder`.
   - Fast-tracked as its own spec, `GATE-INVERSE-ALIAS-V1` (branch
     `spec/gate-inverse-alias-v1`, gated implementation on
     `impl/gate-inverse-alias-v1`).
   - Source: probe E1; gate Q1; construct's world scan.
1. **Identity ignores frames.**
   - Writes: `aliases`/`same_as` on a `knows:`-frame item land in canon.
   - Reads: closure, reconcile, `by_alias`, durable contradictions and `retype`
     read every frame.
   - Probes: a `same_as` asserted in a belief frame merged canon; a name known
     only to Alice drove a canon merge; a canon `retype` retracted a row in
     Marn's frame.
   - Breaks §6 / §18.1 #4.
   - Source: identity P1-1/P1-2; gate P1-3.
2. **A stale same-time tie freezes a location.**
   - A tie inside one source class returns "conflicted" before the classes are
     compared. One gossip line putting someone in two rooms at t=1 pins them
     there through every later narrated move.
   - Reachable by ordinary chunk stamping. Construct moves its player every few
     turns and asked for this one high.
   - Source: folds P1-1.
3. **Evidence rank governs STATE only.**
   - A later *inferred* DISPOSITIONAL silently replaces a *stated* one.
   - CONSTITUTIVE serves an earliest *inferred* kind over a later *stated* one.
   - STATE always beats DISPOSITIONAL whatever the times: after accrual
     promotion, "grumpy" at t=0.5 beats "cheerful" at t=1–3.
   - Breaks P5 and §5.
   - Source: folds P1-2.
4. **`refer()` turns one ambiguity pick into a permanent canon alias.**
   - After one resolution of "the drawer" in the study, "the drawer" in the
     kitchen resolves to the desk drawer forever, in every frame and as-of.
   - Ambiguous reads append two rows each, so the log grows on reads (`ask`
     goes through this path).
   - §9 says such learning is discourse-scoped and short-lived. Construct ranked
     it high: it breaks the object permanence construct promises players.
   - Source: gate P1-2.
6. **Mechanism rows are recognized by attribute name, not authority.**
   - An ingestor row `(a:4 · retracts · …)` retracts a row, through
     `ingest_structured` and `commit_set` alike. It skips every check the typed
     retract applies and can retract inside its own atomic set.
   - A forged `resolved_by` makes `resolve()` serve an unrelated row as a
     thunk's resolution, with zero model calls.
   - Breaks the role-authority matrix (§12, §18.1 #5).
   - Source: core P1-1.
7. **Merge vetoes can be bypassed.**
   - A raw `same_as` through `ingest_structured` ignores both hard vetoes
     (containment, `distinct_from`), and no read reports it.
   - An `aka` chain lets C join A's correlation set although `reject(a, c)` was
     declared.
   - `reconcile` merges `aka`-correlated pairs, which leaks a later reveal
     backwards.
   - Source: identity P1-3/4/5.
8. **`fidelity_audit.open_conflicts` leaks frames and goes stale.**
   - It is read from a stored table that only `truth.scan()` fills. That call is
     below the porcelain and unreachable over MCP.
   - It is not frame-scoped: a canon audit lists a `knows:` conflict, revealing
     the entity.
   - It still lists a conflict after that conflict is resolved, and ignores
     `as_of`.
   - Source: core P1-2; surfaces P1-5.
9. **Time-value hazards.**
   - `valid_from == 0.0` reads as "no time" in four fold sites, and the cursor
     starts at 0.0.
   - A text `valid_from` ("spring 1660") is admitted, then crashes `state`,
     `locate` and `snapshot`.
   - Source: folds P1-4; gate P1-1.
10. **Read side misses what the write side does.**
    - Reads don't canonicalize attributes: `state(ann, "located_in")` says
      unknown.
    - `state()` returns one member of a set-valued key.
    - An unresolved placeholder is reported as `known`.
    - `who_knows` sees only the folded winner. It misses set members, matches an
      accrue delta row instead of the total, and never matches a `$decimal`.
    - Source: surfaces P1-1/2/3; folds Q3.
11. **`ask()` can answer from the future.** The model's plan `as_of` overrides
    the caller's. Source: surfaces P1-4.
12. **World physics isn't persisted.**
    - `policy` isn't stored in the world file. Reopening a tracking world
      without `policy=` runs it as fiction, with no decay and no learned-at
      stamps.
    - The MCP launcher can't set `policy` at all.
    - §16 promises "one file plus its policy row".
    - Source: surfaces P1-6.
13. **Silent drops.**
    - `p.ingest()` never copies its skips into its Receipt.
    - A malformed id in the *value* position (`in: "Place:Cellar"`) is stored as
      text and wins the location fold, so the entity silently loses its place.
    - Source: surfaces trap 4; gate Q2.
14. **Narrower ones.**
    - Salience ignores `asserted_as_of`, so as-of materializations aren't
      reproducible.
    - The durability sidecar can't be rebuilt from the log: resolver/injected
      judgments are lost on `rebuild()` or dump→build.
    - Bookkeeping rows reach the paid classifier.
    - Source: folds P1-5; gate P2-7/P2-9.

## Proposal: five spec batches, in this order

Each batch goes through the founder's loop (spec → pbr GREEN → build → pbr
GREEN, then ship-notice construct). Recommended order: first `GATE-INVERSE-ALIAS-V1`
(live corruption), then the batches that serve wrong answers to playing hosts.
Batch A's spec is drafted (`specs/FOLD-CORRECTNESS-V1.md` r2), with a gated
implementation on `impl/fold-correctness-v1`: 27 oracle tests, 604 green, blast
radius measured on two worlds.

| Batch | Contents | Porcelain impact |
|---|---|---|
| **A. FOLD-CORRECTNESS-V1** | P1 #2, #3, #9; one value-identity comparator (four today disagree on merged ids); private `__containment__` key no longer leaks | Answers change only where they were wrong |
| **B. GATE-AUTHORITY-V1** | P1 #6, #13 (P1 #0 fast-tracked separately); raw `same_as` through the guarded path; side-row authority checked before any write; one `_normalize_item` (string `aliases`, null `frame`, entity-typed integers); attribute declarations validated | Bad inputs now skip with receipts instead of corrupting |
| **C. FRAME-SCOPED-IDENTITY-V1** | P1 #1, #7, #8; `snapshot`'s cross-frame existence leak; `identity_conflicts()` read | One new read; identity results change where frames leaked |

| **E. ## Decisions for Kabe (each with a recommendation)

1. **Is identity perspectival?** Can a merge or alias be true only in one
   character's frame?
   - *Recommend:* `same_as` is canon-only, and the gate refuses it in other
     frames with a receipt. Aliases are frame-scoped (Alice may call Bob "the
     traitor" without canon learning it).
2. **Should `knows:` frames follow merges?** After `merge(dan, daniel)`,
   `knows:person:daniel` is unreadable as `knows:person:dan`.
   - *Recommend:* yes, resolve the frame suffix through identity at read time.
3. **Should a world file carry its own physics?** *Recommend:* persist `policy`
   at creation; on reopen default to it and raise on mismatch.
4. **The durability pin** for host-control attributes (construct's plot:/session:
   rows). Construct has agreed to the shape: `attr:<name> · durability ·
   STATE|DISPOSITIONAL|CONSTITUTIVE`, never EVENT, read before the model.
   - *Recommend:* yes, in batch E.
   - Separately: `fold_policy=last_write|move` is advertised but never read.
     Remove it, or give it that meaning.
5. **Write durability.**
   - Every append outside an atomic unit commits, about 9 ms per row.
   - WAL + `synchronous=NORMAL` would cut that, at the cost of losing the last
     moments on power loss.
   - *Recommend:* leave it as is until a host measures write latency as a
     problem.
6. **The connection facade.** About 120 lines of attribute-proxy guard that any
   holder can get around; the SQLite authorizer is the real enforcement.
   - *Recommend:* simplify to a small gated API in a later batch (pbr reviewed
     the current design, so it goes back to pbr).

## P2: structure worth fixing

- **One gate table instead of eight hand copies.** A merge-veto table with hard
  and soft tiers, evaluated lazily. The `_mergeable` and `_decline_context`
  copies agreed on 400 random pairs, which gives a ready regression test.
- **One "rows between two closures" scan** instead of eight.
- **One kind profile** instead of five readers with three normalizations.
  Mixed case today gets stuck: "Relic" vs "relic" is a kind conflict the gate
  refuses, and `retype` refuses to fix it.
- **Durable contradictions:** stop calling a set-valued attribute a
  contradiction (`connects_to`).
- **`visible(frame=None)` silently means all frames** at 46 of 80 call sites.
  Make `frame` required, with an explicit `ALL_FRAMES`.
- **`indexes.py` is five modules in one file** (1,800 lines: fold, confidence,
  walks/route, awareness reads, wiring). Split it behind the existing facade
  after batch A.
- **Classifier drift.** The single and batch rubrics differ, and there are three
  fallbacks for an ambiguous row. Use one rubric (needs an eval run).
- **Two extraction prompts that have drifted.** Build both from shared pieces.
- **`events()` invents events** from `caused_by` rows, depends on classification
  having run, and parses movement twice (also in `in_transit`). Use one reader.
- **A salience read writes to the database** and the cache grows forever.
  Keep it in memory per read.
- **Facts carry pre-merge ids**, so one entity's facts appear under two ids.
- **The scene cursor resets to 0.0 on reopen.** Documented now; a receipt
  warning would make it visible.

## Do not touch (named by the auditors)

- The two-axis visibility rule in `visible()`; `asserted_at = seq` with the
  contiguity checks and sorted-key dump (the determinism contract).
- `atomic_unit`'s authorizer, owner phases and poison-on-failed-rollback.
- `_fold_over_closure` as the single fold core; `_source_class`'s composite
  source identity; `_time_trust`'s fail-closed branches; the exact-decimal
  accrue path.
- The gate's check ordering (authority raises first, then the malformed-id
  check, identity, intervals, per-edge skips) and the atomic wrapper.
- The classifier's structural ban on EVENT.
- The `extract()`/`ingest_structured()` split.
- Identity's membrane discipline and receipts; `aka` kept out of
  `resolve`/`closure`; `_decline_context` as the single source of decline
  reasons.
- MCP's explicit registry with its completeness test; the required `frame` on
  `entities`/`facts`; `encode_out` on every return.

- `remembrancer.py` and `session.py` hand-fold `facts(knows:…)`. Fix: the
