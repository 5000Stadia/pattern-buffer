# Complete the answer key

Your key.json is accurate in places but incomplete. Graders will score a reading system against it,
so every fact the brief names must be in it. Re-read ch01.md to ch06.md in full and update
key.json (do not change the chapters unless a fix below requires it):

1. **entities**: every named or distinctly described character, place and object that matters, with
   EVERY way the text refers to each (lowercase, letter for letter), and `alias_first_chapter` for
   every alias (the first chapter whose text uses it).
2. **locations**: every place a character or object is said to be, and every move, with from/to
   chapters and an exact evidence quote. A move ends the previous entry (`to_chapter`).
3. **holdings**: every time an object is held, carried, given, stolen or put down, with chapters and
   an exact quote.
4. **containers**: every container whose contents the text states, with the chapter of the reveal.
5. **same_as**: every case where two names or descriptions turn out to be one character or thing;
   **not_same**: every pair a careful reader could confuse (similar names, shared titles, look-alike
   objects), with the reason.
6. **knowledge, false_beliefs, corrections**: every one the text makes clear.

If the story itself has fewer than these from the brief — at least 3 look-alike (not_same) pairs,
at least 2 identity reveals (same_as), at least 3 objects in containers revealed later — revise the
chapters minimally to add them fairly (a careful human reader must be able to follow each), and
key them.

Then check every alias and evidence quote against the text letter for letter and fix any mismatch.
Write CHANGES.md listing what you added or revised.
