# Revision 2 (the last one)

Two independent readers checked your revised novella and key. Their verdicts are in
../verdicts_A.json and ../verdicts_B.json. Read both in full. Each holds a verdict per key entry,
plus `missing` and `text_contradictions` lists. This is the final revision, so fix everything they
raise.

## The text
1. Fix **every** item in both `text_contradictions` lists. Change the prose so each object, room
   and fact has one consistent history. Examples: the rain vial poured out in ch7 must not be full
   in ch10; the blue paper and the salt in ch12; the notice under the mattress vs on the wall;
   the unallotted cup on the sill before Tavin gives it; ch7 mentioning things only found in ch8;
   the tooth and the tin case between ch3 and ch4.
2. Where two different things share a description (the chipped cup, the cracked jar, the notices,
   "the old Listener", "Keeper"), make the prose distinguish them, or keep the shared phrase out
   of the key.
3. Keep the story, the style and the length (about 25,000 words; about 2,000 words a chapter).

## The key
1. Fix every entry either reader marked `wrong` or `unclear`, so that the text settles it.
2. Aliases: list only phrases that name this one entity everywhere they occur in the text. Drop
   generic or shared phrases ("the stone", "letter", "pot", "chair", "small room", "notice",
   "Keeper", "the chief clerk", unless the text ties them to one entity every time).
3. Add the central reveals to same_as: the woman with the copper hand = Vey Orin = the first
   Listener; Or's mother = Mara; the hooded, scarred figure Or remembers = Mara. Each takes the
   alias used before the reveal and the reveal's chapter.
4. Add the false beliefs and lies the readers list, with the chapters they start and end, and
   anything else in their `missing` lists that matters.

that no alias appears in a passage about a different entity. Write CHANGES2.md listing what you
